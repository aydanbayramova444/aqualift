Smart Urban Rainwater Collection & Management

Rain that floods the street becomes a managed resource.

AquaLift collects rainwater that accumulates on streets and public spaces, filters it, stores it underground, moves it with sensor-driven pumps and returns it to the city for non-potable reuse.

Stage A01 / 05
01

Stage AWater Collection System

Purpose

Collect rainwater accumulated on streets, pavements and other urban surfaces.

Runoff follows the natural slope of the street toward the points where it would normally pool — kerb lines, low points of roads, squares and pedestrian areas. AquaLift places specially designed collection points exactly there. Grated inlets admit the water while holding back large objects, linear drainage channels intercept sheet flow across wide paved surfaces, and underground collection chambers gather everything and direct it by gravity into the system.

  • A.2

Stage B02 / 05
02

Stage BFiltration System

Purpose

Remove large debris, leaves, sediments, sand and other unwanted materials from collected rainwater.

Street runoff carries leaves, plastic, sand and fine sediment. Before it is stored, the water passes through a preliminary filtration train. Screens catch floating and coarse debris, separation chambers slow the flow so heavier particles settle out under gravity, and filter media retain the finer material. This protects tanks and pumps from clogging and delivers cleaner water that is ready for storage or further treatment.

Physical components03
  • B.1
    Debris collection filtersScreens and baskets that capture leaves, litter and other coarse material at the entry.
  • B.2
    Sediment separation chambersCalm or vortex chambers in which sand and grit settle to a removable sump.
  • B.3
    Filtration unitsLayered gravel and sand media that hold back fine particles before storage.

Stage C03 / 05
03

Stage CUnderground Storage

Purpose

Temporarily store collected rainwater in underground storage modules.

Filtered water is held in modular tanks installed below streets and public spaces, where they take up no surface area. During heavy rain these modules absorb the peak volume that the existing drainage network cannot accept at once, then release or supply it gradually. This flattens the load on city drains and keeps water off the surface. Level sensors report how full each module is at every moment, giving the control system the data it needs for the next stage.

Physical components03
  • C.1
    Underground modular storage tanksLoad-bearing modules that can be sized to the catchment and extended as needed.
  • C.2
    Water storage chambersSealed chambers that hold water until it is pumped onward or reused.
  • C.3

04

Stage DSmart Pumping System

Purpose

Automatically transfer collected water to suitable drainage infrastructure or designated storage facilities.

Pumps run on sensor data, not manual control. When the level in storage reaches an upper threshold the pumps start; when it falls to a lower threshold they stop. Flow sensors confirm that water is actually moving, so blockages or faults are detected early. Readings are sent to the AquaLift management layer, where AI analyses them over time to optimise pumping — for example, freeing capacity before heavy rainfall or holding water while downstream drains are already under load.

Stage E05 / 05
05

Stage EWater Reuse

Purpose

Reuse collected rainwater for suitable non-potable purposes after appropriate filtration and water quality assessment.

Instead of simply being discharged, part of the collected water becomes a resource for the city. After appropriate filtration and a water quality assessment, it can be supplied wherever drinking-water quality is not required. Every litre used this way is a litre that does not have to come from the municipal supply — turning a problem on the street into value for parks, landscapes and green infrastructure.

AquaLift is an innovative urban water management solution designed to address the problem of rainwater accumulation in streets, residential areas, public spaces and other urban environments.

01 — System

One integrated system

The system combines physical water collection infrastructure, filtration units, underground storage modules, automated pumping mechanisms, IoT sensors and AI-powered management.

CollectionFiltrationStoragePumpingIoTAI
02 — Process

How the water moves

Rainwater accumulated on urban surfaces is collected through specially designed drainage inlets and directed into filtration units, where debris and sediments are separated. The collected water is then transferred to underground storage tanks or safely redirected to suitable drainage infrastructure using automated pumping systems.

03 — Intelligence

Sensors and AI

IoT sensors continuously monitor water levels and flow conditions, while AI analyzes the collected data to optimize pumping operations, monitor system performance and improve water management efficiency.

04 — Reuse

Water as a resource

An additional objective is to reuse collected rainwater for suitable non-potable purposes, such as irrigation and maintaining urban green spaces, following appropriate filtration and water quality assessment.

Impact

By combining physical engineering with intelligent technologies, AquaLift aims to reduce urban water accumulation, improve stormwater management and promote more sustainable use of water resources.
